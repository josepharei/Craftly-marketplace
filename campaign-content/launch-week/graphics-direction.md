# Craftly Launch Week Graphics Concepts

## General Brand Assets to Use:
- **Primary Indigo:** `#6366F1`
- **Emerald Green:** `#10B981`
- **Amber Orange:** `#F59E0B`
- **Font:** Inter (Bold for headlines, Regular for body)

---

## Post 1: The Grand Opening
- **Format:** Square (1080x1080) & Story (1080x1920)
- **Background:** Primary Indigo (#6366F1)
- **Headline:** "CRAFTLY IS LIVE" (Inter Bold, White, Centered)
- **Subheadline:** "The Marketplace for Digital Creators"
- **Visuals:** Use `logo-concept-1.png` prominently. Floating digital asset icons (pixels, pen nibs) in Emerald Green and Amber Orange around the edges.
- **CTA:** "Explore Now" (White button with Indigo text)

---

## Post 2: Seller Feature
- **Format:** Square (1080x1080)
- **Background:** Off-White (#F9FAFB) with Indigo accents.
- **Headline:** "MEET THE PIONEERS"
- **Layout:** A grid showing 3-4 creator profile pictures (placeholders) and their top products.
- **Visuals:** Clean, minimalist borders using Slate Gray (#1F2937).

---

## Post 3: Product Showcase
- **Format:** Carousel (1080x1080)
- **Slide 1:** High-quality mockup of a font ("Modern Sans").
- **Slide 2:** Close-up of the letterforms.
- **Slide 3:** Example of the font in use on a website or poster.
- **Style:** Minimalist, using the product's own aesthetic but framed with Craftly's Indigo branding at the bottom.

---

## Post 4: Why Sell on Craftly?
- **Format:** Twitter Thread Graphic / LinkedIn Single Image
- **Background:** Emerald Green (#10B981) - representing growth/revenue.
- **Headline:** "KEEP MORE OF YOUR REVENUE"
- **Visuals:** Simple comparison chart: Craftly (low commission) vs. Competitor (high commission). Use Amber Orange for the "Craftly" bar to make it pop.

## Post 5: Creator Tip

## Post 6: Why Buy on Craftly?
- **Format:** Story (1080x1920)

# Craftly Social Media Marketing Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
Craftly is a multi-vendor marketplace for digital products. This marketing plan outlines the strategy to attract both high-quality creators (sellers) and digital asset consumers (buyers) through a coordinated social media presence across Instagram, Twitter/X, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Pinterest.

## 2. Goals & Success Metrics (KPIs)
*   **Brand Awareness:** Reach 10,000+ potential users in the first 3 months.
*   **Seller Acquisition:** Onboard 100+ active sellers.
*   **Buyer Conversion:** Achieve a 2% conversion rate from social traffic.
*   **Community Growth:** Build a following of 5,000+ across all platforms.
*   **Engagement Rate:** Maintain an average engagement rate of 3% or higher on Instagram and TikTok.

## 3. Platform Strategy

### Instagram (@CraftlyMarket)
*   **Focus:** Visual inspiration and product showcases.
*   **Content Types:**
    *   **Reels:** "Day in the life" of a creator, product reveals, design tips.
    *   **Carousel Posts:** "Top 5 Fonts for 2024", "How to use our Notion templates".
    *   **Stories:** Behind-the-scenes, polls, "New Arrival" alerts.
*   **Engagement Strategy:** Use interactive stickers, respond to DMs/comments within 2 hours.

### Twitter/X (@CraftlyMarket)
*   **Focus:** Industry news, creator networking, and quick updates.
*   **Content Types:**
    *   **Threads:** "The future of the creator economy", "How to scale your digital product business".
    *   **Engagement:** Replying to big creators in the design space, participating in #DesignTwitter.
    *   **Support:** Directing users to help docs and answering platform questions.

### TikTok (@CraftlyMarket)
*   **Focus:** Short-form, high-energy content and trend-jacking.
*   **Content Types:**
    *   **Trends:** Using trending audio to showcase digital products.
    *   **Educational:** "3 tools every designer needs" (mentioning Craftly).
    *   **Authenticity:** Showing the messy process of creation.

### LinkedIn (Craftly)
*   **Focus:** Professional growth and B2B partnerships.
*   **Content Types:**
    *   **Company Updates:** Milestone celebrations (e.g., 1,000 sales).
    *   **Thought Leadership:** Articles on the gig economy and digital entrepreneurship.
    *   **Recruitment:** Highlighting why sellers should choose Craftly over competitors.

### Pinterest (Craftly)
*   **Focus:** Long-term traffic via high-quality pins.
*   **Content Types:**
    *   **Product Pins:** Direct links to marketplace items.
    *   **Inspiration Boards:** "Website Design Inspo", "Typography Trends".
    *   **Tutorials:** Step-by-step graphic design guides.

## 4. Target Audience
*   **Sellers (The "Supply"):** Digital artists, font designers, template makers, course creators, and passive income seekers.
*   **Buyers (The "Demand"):** Freelance designers, small business owners, social media managers, marketing agencies, and hobbyists.

## 5. Content Pillars
1.  **Creator Spotlights:** Interviews and highlights of top sellers to build trust and community.
2.  **Product Demos:** Showing the products in action to demonstrate value.
3.  **Educational/Value-Add:** Tips for improving design skills or business growth to establish authority.
4.  **Platform News:** New features, commission updates, and events to keep users informed.
5.  **User-Generated Content:** Reposting work from buyers who used Craftly assets to show social proof.

## 8. Influencer Outreach Plan

### Target Influencer Tiers
*   **Micro-influencers (5k - 50k followers):** Higher engagement, niche expertise.
*   **Nano-influencers (1k - 5k followers):** High trust, very affordable/product-exchange based.

### Priority Niches
1.  **Graphic Design:** People who share tutorials on Canva, Photoshop, Illustrator.
2.  **Productivity/Notion:** People who share setups and templates.
3.  **Creative Entrepreneurship:** People who teach others how to sell digital products.
4.  **Web Development:** People who share UI/UX tips and components.

### Outreach Template
"Hi [Name]! Love your work on [Topic]. We're launching Craftly, a new multi-vendor marketplace for digital products, and we'd love to have you as a featured creator/partner. We think your audience would benefit from [Product Type]. Would you be interested in a collaboration? Let us know!"

### Incentives
*   **Early Access:** Let them be the first to list products.
*   **Commission Boost:** Offer a higher revenue share for the first 3 months.
*   **Featured Placement:** Prominent display on the homepage.
*   **Affiliate Commission:** 10% on every sale they refer.

## 9. Growth Tactics
1.  **"First 100 Sellers" Campaign:** Special badges and zero-commission for the first month for the first 100 sellers to sign up.
2.  **Viral Giveaways:** Partner with a seller to give away a "Mega Creative Bundle" (worth $500+) in exchange for email signups and social follows.
3.  **Community Engagement:** Actively participate in subreddits like r/graphicdesign, r/smallbusiness, and r/creatoreconomy.
4.  **SEO-Driven Pins:** Use Pinterest as a visual search engine, optimizing pin descriptions with long-tail keywords.
5.  **Collaborative Posts:** Use Instagram's "Collab" feature with sellers when they list new products.

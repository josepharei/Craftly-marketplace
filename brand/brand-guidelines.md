# Craftly Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview
Craftly is a multi-vendor marketplace for high-quality digital products. Our brand identity reflects creativity, professionalism, and the digital nature of our products.

## Logo
The Craftly logo combines digital elements (pixels/chips) with traditional craft symbols (pens/shapes) to represent the fusion of technology and creativity.

- **Logo Concepts:** [Shared Directory](/home/team/shared/brand/logos/)
    - `logo-concept-1.png`: Stylized 'C' with pixel elements.
    - `logo-concept-2.png`: Pen nib and computer chip fusion.
    - `logo-concept-3.png`: Modern typography-focused design.

## Color Palette
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Primary Indigo | `#6366F1` | Primary branding, buttons, links |
| Emerald Green | `#10B981` | Success states, creative accents |
| Amber Orange | `#F59E0B` | Highlights, notifications, energy |
| Slate Gray | `#1F2937` | Main text, dark UI elements |
| Off White | `#F9FAFB` | Backgrounds, secondary containers |

## Typography
- **Headings:** Inter (Bold/Semi-bold)
- **Body Text:** Inter (Regular/Medium)
- **Code/Tech:** JetBrains Mono or Fira Code

## Visual Style
- **Aesthetic:** Clean, minimalist, and modern.
- **Iconography:** Flat, line-based icons with subtle rounded corners.
- **Mood:** Professional yet inspiring for creators.
- **Moodboard:** [moodboard.png](/home/team/shared/brand/style/moodboard.png)

## Design Principles
1. **Clarity:** The interface should never get in the way of the content (the products).
2. **Trust:** Security and reliability are paramount for a marketplace.
3. **Inspiration:** The brand should motivate creators to list their best work.
